# Harbor Point Lending: Personal Loan Agreement (Template v3.2)

*Synthetic document for testing. Harbor Point Lending is a fictional bank.*

## 1. Parties and Loan

The Lender is Harbor Point Lending Ltd. The Borrower is named in the Schedule. The principal is up to 50,000 USD. The term is between 12 and 84 months.

## 2. Interest Rate

The interest rate is a fixed annual percentage rate (APR) set at origination and stated in the Schedule. The rate does not change during the term.

## 3. Repayment Schedule

Monthly instalments are due on the 5th day of each month. Each instalment covers interest first and then principal.

## 4. Prepayment

### 4.1 Partial prepayment

The Borrower may prepay up to 20% of the original principal in each calendar year without penalty. Partial prepayments reduce the instalment amount or the term, at the Borrower's choice.

### 4.2 Full prepayment penalty

If the Borrower repays the whole loan early, a penalty applies to the outstanding principal:

- Full prepayment in months 0 to 36: a penalty of 2% of the outstanding principal.
- Full prepayment after month 36: a penalty of 1% of the outstanding principal.

### 4.3 Penalty waivers

No penalty applies when the loan is refinanced with Harbor Point Lending, or when the prepayment is funded by an insurance payout that the Borrower can document.

### 4.4 Notice of prepayment

The Borrower must give written notice of a full prepayment at least 10 business days before the prepayment date. The notice must state the intended payoff date.

## 5. Late Payments

An instalment not received within 10 days of its due date is late. A late fee of 5% of the overdue instalment is charged after the 10-day grace period.

## 6. Default

The Lender may declare default if an instalment is more than 60 days late. On default, the full outstanding balance becomes due. Default interest may be charged at the rate stated in the Schedule plus 3%.

## 7. Governing Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the jurisdiction stated in the Schedule.
