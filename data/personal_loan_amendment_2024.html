Amendment notice: personal loan prepayment terms

Effective date: 1 July 2024. Applies to: personal loans originated on or after this date.

What changes

This notice amends section 4.2 of Personal Loan Agreement Template v3.2 for the loans described above.

  • Full prepayment in months 0 to 24: a penalty of 1.5% of the outstanding principal.
  • Full prepayment after month 24: the penalty is waived (0%).

What does not change

The annual partial prepayment allowance of 20% of original principal stays the same. The 10 business day written notice requirement in section 4.4 also stays the same.

Loans originated before 1 July 2024

Those loans keep the terms of section 4.2 of Template v3.2 for their full term.
