HARBOR POINT HOME LOANS: MORTGAGE TERMS (2023 EDITION)
Synthetic document for testing. Harbor Point Home Loans is a fictional lender.

Section 1. Loan Overview
The mortgage is secured on the property named in the offer. The term is up to 30 years. Monthly payments include interest and principal.

Section 2. Rate Types
Customers choose either a fixed-rate mortgage or a variable-rate mortgage. The fixed period is set in the offer.

Section 7. Early Repayment Charges

7.1 Fixed-rate mortgages.
An early repayment charge applies when all or part of a fixed-rate mortgage is repaid during the fixed period. The charge is a percentage of the amount repaid:
  - Year 1 and year 2: 3% of the amount repaid.
  - Year 3: 2% of the amount repaid.
  - Year 4: 1% of the amount repaid.
  - From year 5: no charge.

7.2 Variable-rate mortgages.
There is no early repayment charge on a variable-rate mortgage at any time.

7.3 Overpayment allowance.
Each year, the customer may overpay up to 10% of the outstanding balance without an early repayment charge. Any overpayment above this allowance is charged as in section 7.1.

7.4 Porting.
A fixed-rate mortgage may be ported to a new property if the lender approves the new property. No early repayment charge applies to a port that is completed within 90 days of the sale of the old property.

Section 8. Arrears
If a payment is missed, the lender contacts the customer. Arrears interest is charged at the mortgage rate plus 2%.
